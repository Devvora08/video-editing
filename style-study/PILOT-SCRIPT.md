# Pilot script: "Why most no-code apps make $0"

About 200 words, roughly 75 to 85 seconds at a relaxed pace. Read only the **spoken lines**. The *[visual]* notes show what each line gives the animation to work with.

---

**Every single day, around ten thousand new apps get launched.**
*[number counts up to 10,000 · flood of app icons]*

**And almost ninety percent of them never make a single dollar.**
*[icons grey out one by one · "90%" slam · $0 badge]*

**I know, because I built three of those.**
*[three app cards → each gets a red ✕]*

**So what goes wrong? It's not the tools. Bubble, Webflow, Claude, they all work.**
*[tool icon row builds left to right · green ✓ on each]*

**The problem is the order you do things in.**
*[chapter card: "THE REAL PROBLEM"]*

**Most people go: idea, then build, then launch… and then they look for customers.**
*[4-step flow chain draws in · last step flickers with a "?"]*

**By then, they've spent three months building something nobody asked for.**
*[calendar pages flip · 3 months · confused character]*

**Flip it.**
*[the chain physically reverses]*

**Customers first. Find ten people with a painful problem. Talk to them. Get one of them to pay you before you build anything.**
*[10 person icons → 1 highlights → coin drops in]*

**Then build only the smallest thing that solves it.**
*[big app box shrinks to a tiny card]*

**That one change took my next app from zero to its first hundred dollars in three weeks.**
*[line chart climbs · $0 → $100 counter · "3 WEEKS" badge]*

**In this series, I'm doing it live: zero to one thousand dollars a month, with no code.**
*[progress bar $0 ──── $1K · series title card]*

**Let's go.**

---

Recording tips:
- Swap in your real numbers and stories wherever they differ (the three apps, $100 in three weeks). True details make better visuals.
- Leave a short breath between lines; I'll cut the gaps.
- Use any quiet room and a phone close to your mouth. A WAV or MP3 dropped into the project root is fine.
